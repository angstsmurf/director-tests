Made from T_COLR01 with vwscpatch.py (~/dos/d4tools) and SCORE.PY, then
the scripts set from MOVIE.TXT (cast 1) and FRAME.TXT (cast 3) in real
D4. Frames 2-7: channels 1-4 hold the ARGYLE bitmap (cast 2). The score
moves channels 2-4 right in every frame (locH only):
  ch2 x 61 200 260 300 340 380   moveable except in frame 5
  ch3 x 61 200 260 300 340 380   never moveable
  ch4 x 61 120 180 240 300 360   moveable except in frame 5
Frame 3 also sets the blend byte of channels 2-4 to 50 (unused with
copy ink; frame 4 clears it), so those channels differ in more than
their location. Frame 2's script sets the loc of sprite 2 to 150,99.

Real D4 for Windows: 10/10, and screenshot T_SPRT14 (frame 3).
  frame 3: 2 at 150,99, 3 at 200,137, 4 at 120,176
  frame 4: 2 at 150,99, 3 at 260,137, 4 at 120,176
  frame 5: 2 at 300,99, 3 at 300,137, 4 at 240,176
  frame 7: 2 at 380,99, 3 at 380,137, 4 at 360,176
A moveable sprite keeps a drag position over the score location. Lingo
(or a drag) sets it at once; otherwise it is taken from the score one
frame after the sprite becomes moveable (sprite 4 follows the score in
frame 3, then stays at 120 in frame 4; both follow again in frame 7
after being moveable again from frame 6). It is dropped in a frame where
the sprite isn't moveable. Moveable sprites aren't read in frames 2 and
6, the first exitFrame after they become moveable: D4 for Windows
returns 0,-32768 there (see T_SPRT12 notes; Mac D4 returns the loc).

Side finding, kept out of the asserts: an earlier version set sprite 2
to 150,200 and the non-moveable sprite 3 to 150,250. Real D4 then read
sprite 3 at 200,250 in frame 3 and sprite 2 at 300,200 in frame 5: a
Lingo change to a non-puppet survives in every field the next score
frame leaves unchanged (the score's delta bytes seem to be applied over
the live channel). ScummVM replaces the whole sprite (200,137 and
300,99).
