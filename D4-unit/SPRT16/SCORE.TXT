Made from T_COLR01 with vwscpatch.py (~/dos/d4tools) and SCORE.PY, then
the scripts set from MOVIE.TXT (cast 1) and FRAME.TXT (cast 3) in real
D4. Frames 2-7: channels 1-4 hold the ARGYLE bitmap (cast 2), none
moveable, none a puppet. Every frame the score changes only:
  ch2 locH  61 200 260 300 340 380   (locV 99)
  ch3 locV 137 140 143 146 149 152   (locH 61)
  ch4 locH  61 120 180 240 300 360   (locV 176)
  ch4 foreColor 255 -> 35 in frame 4, ink 0 -> 36 in frame 5
Frame 2's script sets the loc of sprites 2 and 3 to 150,250 and the ink
of sprite 4 to 8 and its backColor to 5. Frame 6 jumps to itself once
(go to the frame).

Real D4 for Windows: 10/10.
  frame 3: 2 at 200,250, 3 at 150,140, 4 ink/fore/back 8,255,5
  frame 4: 2 at 260,250, 3 at 150,143, 4 ink/fore/back 8,35,0
  frame 5: 4 ink/fore/back 36,35,0
  frame 6 after the jump: 2 at 340,99, 3 at 61,149, 4 36,35,0
Stepping to the next frame writes the score's delta over the live
channel, so a Lingo change to a non-puppet survives in every field the
delta leaves alone; locH and locV count separately. The delta is made
of whole words, so the foreColor change in frame 4 also writes the
backColor byte next to it and the Lingo backColor is lost. A jump
(even to the same frame) rebuilds the channel from the score.

Covers ScummVM's split of the location copy-back mask into locH and
locV (Sprite::replaceFrom).
  with the split:     10/10
  without the split:  6/10 (sprite 2 back at locV 99, sprite 3 at locH 61
                      in frames 3 and 4)
