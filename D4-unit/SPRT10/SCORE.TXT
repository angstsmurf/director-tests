Made from T_COLR01 (channels 1-4: the bitmap in cast 2, stacked). New
scripts in cast 1 (movie, with the place handler), cast 3 (frame 2) and
cast 4 (frame 3). Phases use copies of cast 2 in slots 9-14. D4
screenshots are in d4shots/.

Real D4 for Windows: erase clears only the LOWEST-NUMBERED channel that
shows the member; the others keep stale pixels.
  P1 (1-4 show it)                 -> channel 1 cleared
  P2 (2-4 show it)                 -> channel 2 cleared
  P3 (3-4 show it)                 -> channel 3 cleared
  P4 (1-4, channels 1 and 4 swapped vertically) -> channel 1 (now at
     the bottom) cleared: by channel number, not screen position.

puppetSprite 2), so sprite 2 sits 60 px left of the others in every phase
(its rect in P1 is 44,83,76,115). ScummVM reads 61, so its sprite 2 lines
up with the others. Not investigated.
