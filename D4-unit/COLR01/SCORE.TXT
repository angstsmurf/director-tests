Made from T_SPRT02. Frames 1-3: channels 1-4 hold cast 2, the ARGYLE
bitmap, with the score's foreColor 255 and backColor 0. The script
channel holds cast 3 (frame 2) and cast 4 (frame 3). Cast 1 is the
movie script. Cast 5 is the field from T_TEXT01 (cast 10 there),
pasted in with copyToClipBoard/pasteClipBoardInto, foreColor 255 and
backColor 0. Casts 6 and 7 are rectangle shapes drawn with the Tools
window; only 6 is used. Neither is in the score.

Real D4 for Windows gives the same 21/21 at 8 and 24 bits (ET4000
with the stock driver; S3 with vbesvga.drv at Depth=24, colorDepth
24; the first 12 asserts also passed at 16 bits). Every index 0-255
reads back unchanged for the sprite foreColor and backColor, the
stageColor, and the field's member foreColor and backColor. Sprite
colors wrap to a byte (300 is 44, -1 is 255), and so does the
stageColor (300 is 44). Bitmap and shape members report foreColor 0
and backColor 255 and ignore sets (7, 9 and 300 all read back 0/255),
since their colors live on the sprite. The last 9 asserts were added
with the LINGO.INI job runner (COLR01 was rebuilt from FRAME2.TXT).

Covers ScummVM c1178978120 (director-jewels; 7a67e32c812 on
director-daedalus), which maps stored RGB colors back to palette
indices in the getters, 89a76b2a675 (the stageColor wraps to a byte
before D7) and 837418aa841 (bitmap and shape member colors are 0/255
and read-only before D7). Run with true_color=true and in 8-bit: with
all three 21/21 in both. With c1178978120 reverted 0/12 of the first
twelve in true color (the getters return RGB565 values such as 65535
and 2047). With 89a76b2a675 reverted only "stageColor 300 wraps to 44"
fails (ScummVM kept 300; 237 in true color). With 837418aa841 reverted
six member asserts fail in both modes (ScummVM read bitmap 0 0 and
shape 255 0 in 8-bit, bitmap 255 255 and shape 255 0 in true color).
